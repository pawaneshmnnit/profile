About

I am a Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology (MNNIT) Allahabad. My research focuses on embodied AI, computer vision, and multimodal learning — particularly on enabling AI systems to understand human activities from first-person (egocentric) visual observations for timely, context-aware assistance.

My recent work spans egocentric action recognition and anticipation, multimodal (RGB + depth) representation learning, and lightweight architectures for video understanding.
